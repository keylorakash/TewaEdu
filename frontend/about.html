About TEWA

Guiding Your Path to Success in Japan

TEWA Education Consultancy is a leading Japanese education and language consultancy based in Suryabinayak-04, Bhaktapur, Nepal, dedicated to helping students achieve their academic and professional aspirations in Japan. Established in 2024, TEWA provides comprehensive support through high-quality Japanese language training, test preparation, educational counseling, course selection guidance, admissions assistance, and documentation services.

Our Mission

To provide quality Japanese language education and professional guidance that helps students achieve their academic and career goals in Japan.

Our Vision

To be a trusted Japanese language and education consultancy, empowering students to succeed globally.

With a student-centered approach and a commitment to excellence, TEWA serves as a trusted partner throughout every stage of the journey—from language learning and university applications to relocation and long-term success in Japan.

"At TEWA, we believe that every dream deserves the right direction, and we are proud to support our students as lifelong members of the TEWA family."

What is Kaigo?

Kaigo (介護) refers to the caregiving sector in Japan, one of the most in-demand and well-compensated industries. Our exclusive Kaigo Scholarship Program provides exceptional opportunities for students seeking both education and employment in this thriving field.

Program Benefits:

Full Scholarship Coverage
